**Added:**

* Added ``convex_hull()`` to ``flatsurf.geometry.euclidean``, an exact convex
  hull of points in the plane, optionally with the points on its boundary.
* Added ``is_convex_polygon()``, ``is_simple_polygon()``,
  ``polygon_point_position()``, and ``polygon_corners()`` to
  ``flatsurf.geometry.euclidean``; they work on a sequence of vertices and
  back the corresponding methods of Euclidean polygons.
