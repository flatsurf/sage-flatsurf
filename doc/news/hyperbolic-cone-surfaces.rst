**Added:**

* Added compact hyperbolic polygons, ``HyperbolicPolygon``, made by
  ``HyperbolicPlane.polygon(vertices=...)``; their angles, in multiples of
  ``2π``, and their area, in units of ``2π``, are exact when rational.
* Added ``HyperbolicIsometryGroup``, the group of isometries of the
  hyperbolic plane as matrices of ``O(2, 1)⁺``, with
  ``tangent_vector_isometry()``, ``segment_isometry()`` and
  ``psl2_matrix()``. Isometries act on points and multiply over the pushout
  of mixed base rings.
* Added ``fixed_point()`` of a hyperbolic isometry and
  ``segment_midpoint()``.
* Added hyperbolic cone surfaces, ``MutableOrientedHyperbolicSurface`` in the
  category ``HyperbolicConeSurfaces``, with ``edge_transformation()`` and
  ``edge_matrix()``. ``glue()`` refuses a gluing whose isometry is not
  defined over the base ring of the surface.
* Added ``change_ring()`` of hyperbolic surfaces and
  ``set_immutable(minimal_field=True)``, which moves a surface to the
  number field generated by the Klein coordinates of its vertices and by its
  side pairings.
* Added the fundamental group of polygonal surfaces, flat and hyperbolic,
  ``fundamental_group()``, with ``vertex_loops()`` and ``boundary_loops()``.
* Added ``holonomy_representation()`` of cone surfaces, flat and
  hyperbolic, and the ``invariant_trace_field()`` of the representation.
* Added ``cyclic_quotient(m)`` of Euclidean and hyperbolic polygons, a
  fundamental domain of a rotation of order ``m`` that leaves the polygon
  invariant.
* Added ``orbifold_points()`` and ``is_orbifold()`` of immutable cone
  surfaces, and ``boundary_components()`` of polygonal surfaces.
* Added a quadratic form ``form=`` to ``HyperbolicPlane``, whose Klein model
  is the ellipse ``x^2 + b y^2 < c``; a plane with another form than
  ``x^2 + y^2 - z^2`` works in Klein coordinates only.
* Added ``HyperbolicPlane.isomorphism()`` between planes of different forms,
  which carries points, polygons, isometries and surfaces, and
  ``standard_form_isometry()`` to the plane of ``x^2 + y^2 - z^2``, through
  which ``psl2_matrix()`` works with any form.
* Exported ``SurfaceSignature`` and ``MutableOrientedHyperbolicSurface``
  from ``flatsurf``.

**Changed:**

* Changed ``replace_polygon()`` to refuse the replacement on an immutable
  surface, flat surfaces included.
