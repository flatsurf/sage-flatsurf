**Added:**

* Added ``SurfaceSignature`` and the ``signature()`` of cone surfaces, flat
  and hyperbolic: the genus, the orders of the cone points, the number of
  cusps and the traces of the boundary components. It provides the topological
  Euler characteristic ``euler_characteristic()``, the divisor Euler
  characteristic of Troyanov ``divisor_euler_characteristic()``, and the
  orbifold orders ``orbifold_orders()`` (Katok's signature) of an orbifold.
