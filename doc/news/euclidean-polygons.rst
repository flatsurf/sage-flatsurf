**Added:**

* Added ``flatsurf.euclidean_polygons``, the counterpart of
  ``flatsurf.hyperbolic_polygons``, with ``square()``, ``rectangle()``,
  ``triangle()`` and ``regular_ngon()``. ``triangle()`` takes its angles as
  multiples of `2\pi` or as proportions. Each constructor takes the keyword
  ``base_ring``.
