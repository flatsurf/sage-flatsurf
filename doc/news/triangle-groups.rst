**Added:**

* Added ``fuchsian_groups.triangle()``, the cocompact triangle group with
  given periods as a matrix group of ``SO(2, 1)``, generated by the rotations
  about the vertices of a triangle, of the given orders and of product the
  identity. The triangle is placed so that its coordinates generate a field
  of the smallest degree allowed by the invariant trace field and the
  quaternion algebra of the group, e.g. the group ``(2, 3, 7)`` is defined
  over a field of degree 6.
