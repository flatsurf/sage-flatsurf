**Added:**

* Added ``fuchsian_groups.triangle()``, the cocompact triangle group with
  given periods, generated by the rotations about the vertices of a
  triangle, of the given orders and of product the identity. The group is a
  subgroup of the isometries of the hyperbolic plane
  of the invariant quadratic form of the triangle, over the invariant trace
  field of the group, e.g. the group ``(2, 3, 7)`` is defined over the cubic
  field of ``x^3 - 7*x - 7``. With ``standard=True``, the group acts on the
  plane of ``x^2 + y^2 - z^2``, over a field of up to twice that degree.
  ``hyperbolic_polygons.triangle()`` builds its triangle in the same plane.
