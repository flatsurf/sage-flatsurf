**Added:**

* Added ``flatsurf.hyperbolic_polygons``, which builds compact hyperbolic
  polygons exactly from their angles (multiples of `2\pi`) and the hyperbolic
  cosine of some of their sides: ``triangle()``, ``regular_ngon()``,
  ``right_angled_hexagon()``, ``right_angled_pentagon()``, and
  ``right_angled_quadrilateral()``. The triangle and the right-angled
  polygons are built in the plane of a quadratic form of their own, over the
  field of the squared cosines of their angles and hyperbolic cosines of
  their given sides, the invariant trace field of its triangle group for a
  triangle; over ``QQ`` the form has squarefree integer coefficients. The
  regular polygon is defined over the number field generated by the Klein
  coordinates of its vertices. The keyword ``base_ring`` selects another
  ring.
* Added the keyword ``angles`` to ``HyperbolicPlane.polygon()``, which builds
  a hyperbolic triangle from its three angles in the plane of its invariant
  quadratic form, carried to the given plane when the forms differ.
