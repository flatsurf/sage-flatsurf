**Added:**

* Added ``flatsurf.hyperbolic_polygons``, which builds compact hyperbolic
  polygons exactly from their angles (multiples of `2\pi`) and the hyperbolic
  cosine of some of their sides: ``triangle()``, ``regular_ngon()``,
  ``right_angled_hexagon()``, ``right_angled_pentagon()``, and
  ``right_angled_quadrilateral()``. ``triangle()`` builds the triangle in the
  plane of its invariant quadratic form, over the invariant trace field of its
  triangle group; the other polygons are defined over the number field
  generated by the Klein coordinates of their vertices. The keyword
  ``base_ring`` selects another ring.
* Added the keyword ``angles`` to ``HyperbolicPlane.polygon()``, which builds
  a hyperbolic triangle from its three angles in the plane of its invariant
  quadratic form, carried to the given plane when the forms differ.
