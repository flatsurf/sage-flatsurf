**Added:**

* Added ``flatsurf.hyperbolic_polygons``, which builds compact hyperbolic
  polygons exactly from their angles (multiples of `2\pi`) and the hyperbolic
  cosine of some of their sides: ``triangle()``, ``regular_ngon()``,
  ``right_angled_hexagon()``, ``right_angled_pentagon()``, and
  ``right_angled_quadrilateral()``. By default, a polygon is defined over the
  number field generated by the Klein coordinates of its vertices.
* Added the keyword ``angles`` to ``HyperbolicPlane.polygon()``, which builds
  a hyperbolic triangle from its three angles over the base ring of the
  plane.
